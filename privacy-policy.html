Privacy Policy

Last updated: (loading)

Community Secondary School, Ido ("the School", "we", "us") operates this Result Checker Portal to let students securely view their academic results and to help school administrators manage academic records. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is protected. Because most users of this portal are students under the age of 18, we've written it to be clear for parents and guardians as well.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and store the following information. It is entered by school administrators; students and parents do not create accounts or submit data themselves:

  • Student records: full name, admission number, class, gender, date of birth, house, attendance, and a passport photograph.
  • Student access credentials: a system-generated 4-digit PIN, used together with the admission number to view a result. This PIN is not a password chosen by the student and does not unlock anything beyond viewing their own published results.
  • Academic records: subject scores, continuous assessment and examination marks, grades, class position, attendance, and teacher/principal remarks.
  • Administrator accounts: the email address and password of school staff authorized to manage the portal, handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (we never see or store the password itself).
  • School settings: the school's name, motto, logo, address, phone number, email, and principal's name, shown publicly across the site.

We do not use cookies, third-party trackers, or advertising scripts. The portal does not ask students for any information directly; everything is entered by school staff.

2. How We Use This Information

  • To let a student look up and view their own term result using their admission number, class, PIN, session and term.
  • To let administrators manage students, teachers, classes, subjects, sessions and results.
  • To automatically calculate totals, averages, grades and class positions.
  • To display school branding (name, logo, motto, contact details) consistently across every page.

We do not sell, rent, or use this information for advertising, and we do not share it with any third party except the infrastructure provider described below.

3. Where Data Is Stored

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for records, Firebase Authentication for administrator sign-in). Firebase is a data processor acting on the School's instructions and maintains its own security and compliance standards; you can review Google's practices in the Firebase Privacy and Security policy. Passport photographs and the school logo are stored as part of student and settings records within the same database.

4. How We Protect Information

  • Only authenticated school administrators can add, edit, publish, or delete records. This is enforced by Firebase security rules, not just by the interface.
  • A result is visible to a student only when it has been explicitly published, and only when the correct admission number, class and PIN are all provided together. Knowing just one of these is not enough.
  • Administrator accounts are protected by Firebase Authentication, with password reset handled securely by email.

5. Children's Privacy

Because this portal is built for a secondary school, most student records belong to minors. We collect only what is necessary to identify a student and display their academic result. We do not collect contact details, location data, or any information beyond what a school ordinarily keeps in its own academic records. Student data is entered and managed exclusively by school staff, not by children directly. Parents or guardians who have questions about a specific student's data should contact the school office using the details below.

6. Data Retention

Student and result records are kept for as long as they are needed for the student's academic history, consistent with the School's normal record-keeping practices. School administrators can edit or delete student and result records directly from the dashboard at any time.

7. Your Rights

A student, parent, or guardian may request to see what information is held about a student, request a correction of inaccurate information, or request deletion of a record, by contacting the school office using the details below. School administrators can act on these requests directly through the Student and Result Management screens.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the portal changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the portal after a change means you accept the updated policy.
